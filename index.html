The Curriculum

Four weeks. One live website. Zero fluff.

Week 01

How the Web Actually Works

  • What happens when you open a website
  • HTML: structure, semantic tags, accessibility
  • Writing your first real webpage
  • Tools setup (VS Code, browser DevTools)

▸ Project: Your personal bio page

Week 02

Making It Look Good

  • CSS fundamentals: box model, flexbox
  • Typography, colour, and spacing
  • Mobile-first responsive design
  • Why your layout breaks (and how to fix it)

▸ Project: Style your bio page

Week 03

Making It Interactive

  • JavaScript basics: variables, functions, events
  • DOM manipulation — what it means and why it matters
  • Adding real behaviour to your page
  • Debugging without panicking

▸ Project: Dark mode toggle or contact form

Week 04

Putting It All Together

  • Git basics: saving and tracking your work
  • Deploying to the internet (Netlify/GitHub Pages)
  • Building your portfolio page
  • What to learn next — an honest roadmap

▸ Project: Live portfolio — your name on the internet
